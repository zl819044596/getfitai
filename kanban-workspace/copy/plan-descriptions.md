## Upper Body Push

| Field | Copy |
|-------|------|
| **Card Headline** | Upper Body Push |
| **Card Subtitle** | 10 min • Intermediate |
| **Card Tagline** | Push-ups, dips & plank variations for a stronger upper body |
| **Card CTA** | Start Workout |
| **Detail Meta** | 8 steps • 6 exercises • 10 minutes |
| **Difficulty Badge** | Intermediate |

### Value Proposition (1-2 sentences, for card tooltip / hover / detail section)

> Build real upper body strength with nothing but your bodyweight. Push through push-ups, dips, and plank variations in 10 focused minutes — no equipment, no gym, no excuses.

### Target Audience

Home exercisers who want visible upper body definition without buying equipment. Anyone who's plateaued on push-ups and wants a structured progression. Post-run / post-cardio add-on for balanced training.

### Key Differentiators

- Only upper body dedicated plan in GetFitAI's library (unique vs Full Body Burn, Core Crusher, Quick Cardio)
- Two rounds per movement build strength endurance, not just spark
- Pike push-ups target shoulders — an area most home workouts neglect
- Plank finisher tests core under fatigue
- Total active time: ~7:30 of work in 10 minutes = high density

### Exercise Sequence (for reference)

1. Arm Circles (30s) — warmup
2. Shoulder Rolls (25s) — warmup
3. Push-Ups (40s + 25s rest) × 2
4. Tricep Dips (40s + 25s rest) × 2
5. Pike Push-Ups (35s + 20s rest) × 2
6. Shoulder Taps (30s + 15s rest) × 2
7. Push-Ups burnout (30s)
8. Plank Hold (30s) — finisher

## Lower Body Strength

| Field | Copy |
|-------|------|
| **Card Headline** | Lower Body Strength |
| **Card Subtitle** | 12 min • Intermediate |
| **Card Tagline** | Squats, lunges, glute bridges & more for powerful legs |
| **Card CTA** | Start Workout |
| **Detail Meta** | 9 steps • 7 exercises • 12 minutes |
| **Difficulty Badge** | Intermediate |

### Value Proposition (1-2 sentences)

> Build lower body strength from the ground up — squats, lunges, glute bridges, and isometric holds that target every major leg muscle. 12 minutes of focused work for stronger, more powerful legs, all at home.

### Target Audience

Runners wanting complementary strength work. General fitness users who skip leg day because they don't have access to a gym. Anyone seeking functional lower body strength for daily life (stairs, carrying, standing endurance).

### Key Differentiators

- Longest plan at 12min → signals substantial strength work (legitimacy)
- Two rounds with deep rest (20s) — allows quality reps, not just cardio in disguise
- Slow calf raises + wall sit finisher target neglected stabilizer muscles
- Glute bridges with pause — feels different from generic "leg day" plans
- Covers quads, hamstrings, glutes, calves, lower back (supermans) — complete chain

### Exercise Sequence (for reference)

1. Bodyweight Squats (slow) (30s) — warmup
2. Leg Swings (25s) — warmup
3. Squats (45s + 20s rest) × 2
4. Lunges (45s + 20s rest) × 2
5. Glute Bridges (40s + 20s rest) × 2
6. Supermans (35s + 15s rest) × 2
7. Calf Raises (30s + 15s rest) × 2
8. Wall Sit (30s) — finisher
9. Glute Bridges pulse hold (30s) — slow finisher

### Value Proposition (1-2 sentences)

> Recover smarter with this full-body flexibility flow. From neck rolls to lying spinal twist — 8 minutes of continuous stretching that moves from standing to the floor, releasing tension in every major muscle group.

### Target Audience

Post-workout cool-down for any other plan in the library. Recovery days between intense sessions. Beginners new to stretching. Desk workers needing full-body tension release. Evening wind-down routine users.

### Key Differentiators

- Only stretch-dedicated plan in GetFitAI's library (no competitor plans offer flexibility-only content)
- Continuous flow — no rest timers, no interruptions (feels like a yoga class, not a tabata timer)
- Standing → ground progression mimics a commercial stretch session
- All exercises have video guidance — no placeholder icons
- Appropriate for rank beginners (All Levels badge is genuine, not aspirational)
- Neck rolls + shoulder stretch target the "tech neck" / desk worker tension pattern

### Exercise Sequence (for reference)

1. Neck Rolls (30s) — warmup
2. Shoulder Stretch (30s) — each side
3. Chest Stretch (30s)
4. Standing Toe Touch (40s)
5. Quad Stretch (40s) — each side
6. Hamstring Stretch (40s) — each side
7. Cat-Cow (30s)
8. Child's Pose (30s) — cooldown
9. Downward Dog (30s) — cooldown
10. Lying Spinal Twist (30s) — each side

## HIIT Blast

| Field | Copy |
|-------|------|
| **Card Headline** | HIIT Blast |
| **Card Subtitle** | 7 min • Intermediate |
| **Card Tagline** | 40/15 intervals — jumping jacks to star jumps, all-out finish |
| **Card CTA** | Start Workout |
| **Detail Meta** | 6 steps • 6 exercises • 7 minutes |
| **Difficulty Badge** | Intermediate |

### Value Proposition (1-2 sentences)

> The shortest, most intense plan in GetFitAI's library — 7 minutes of 40/15 intervals that torch calories and spike your heart rate. Jumping jacks, burpees, high knees, squat jumps, mountain climbers... then an all-out star jump finish.

### Target Audience

Time-crunched users who want maximum results in minimum time. Fat-loss focused athletes supplementing with HIIT. Existing plan users looking for a short finisher after strength work. Users who find 15-minute cardio too long for a morning session.

### Key Differentiators

- Shortest plan in the library at 7min (✓ Quick Cardio is 5min but HIIT Blast is higher intensity)
- 40/15 work/rest = classic HIIT protocol with proven fat-burn research behind it
- Star jumps finisher with no rest — memorable peak moment
- No warmup needed — first round doubles as warm-in
- All exercises are familiar and require zero technique coaching
- Highest calorie-burn-per-minute of any GetFitAI plan

### Exercise Sequence (for reference)

1. Jumping Jacks (40s + 15s rest) — warm-in
2. Burpees (40s + 15s rest)
3. High Knees (40s + 15s rest)
4. Squat Jumps (35s + 15s rest)
5. Mountain Climbers (40s + 15s rest)
6. Star Jumps (30s) — all-out finisher, no rest
